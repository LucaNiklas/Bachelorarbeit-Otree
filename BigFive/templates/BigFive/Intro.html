Welcome

The entire session will take approximately 5 to 12 minutes to complete.

In the first part, you will take the Big Five Personality Test, where you’ll be asked to rate how well certain statements describe you.

After completing this section, you will play up to 10 rounds of Blackjack. Further instructions for the game will be provided right before it begins.

Payout: Everyone who completes all 10 rounds enters a raffle for the prize of 150€. Your chance of being selected is proportional to your final balance — higher balances improve your odds. Lower balances remain eligible and can still be drawn. Only those who exit early or end below the minimum bet size of 5 points are excluded.
