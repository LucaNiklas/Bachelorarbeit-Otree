Blackjack – Game Rules

  • Your goal is to get as close as possible to 21 points without going over.
  • Number cards count at face value, face cards (J, Q, K) count 10, and Aces count as 1 or 11.
  • You start with two cards; the dealer also gets two, one of which is face down.
  • You can choose to:
    • Hit – draw another card
    • Stand – keep your current hand
    • Double – double your bet and receive one final card
  • If your total exceeds 21, you bust and lose immediately.
  • After your turn, the dealer reveals their hidden card and draws until reaching 17 or more.
  • A Blackjack (Ace + 10-value card) beats all other hands.
  • A regular win pays an amount equal to your bet.
  • A Blackjack pays 3 to 2.
  • If you and the dealer have the same total, it’s a push (no win or loss).
