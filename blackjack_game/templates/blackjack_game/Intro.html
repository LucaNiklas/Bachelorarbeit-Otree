Blackjack – Game Rules

Below you will find a compact overview of the rules you will use during the Blackjack rounds.

  • Your goal is to reach a hand value as close as possible to 21 without going over.
  • Number cards count at face value, face cards (J, Q, K) count 10, and Aces count as 1 or 11.
  • You and the dealer each start with two cards; one of the dealer’s cards is hidden.
  • On your turn you may:
    • Hit – draw another card.
    • Stand – keep your current hand.
    • Double – double your bet and receive one final card.
  • If your total exceeds 21, you bust and lose the round immediately.
  • After you stand (or bust), the dealer reveals the hidden card and draws until reaching at least 17.
  • An initial Ace plus any 10-value card is a Blackjack and beats all other hands.
  • A regular win pays an amount equal to your bet; a Blackjack pays 3 to 2.
  • If you and the dealer end up with the same total, the outcome is a push (no win or loss).
